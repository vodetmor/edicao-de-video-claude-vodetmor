# After Effects build prompt

Use this after reviewing the storyboard. Attach the approved design and keep the timed beat sheet and exact-copy list in the task.

```text
Build the approved storyboard in Adobe After Effects using the connected Higgsfield local After Effects bridge.

First inspect the current project and verify the live connection. Use the integration's supplied instructions and available operations. Save a separate version and preserve existing work.

Match the approved visual design, exact copy, object identities, timing and cause-and-effect sequence. Use the specified duration, resolution and frame rate.

Prepare only the missing source artwork needed for independently moving elements. Keep one consistent design across those assets. Use native editable text and shapes for typography, UI, drawn lines and simple graphics. Use separate imported images where material detail or illustration calls for them; identify which surfaces remain raster artwork. Do not simply animate the whole storyboard sheet as a flat image.

Build purposeful motion: stagger related arrivals, reveal text after its container lands, animate clear anticipation/contact/settling, and preserve reading holds. Use smooth deliberate camera moves and keep important text unobstructed. Maintain the approved character and product-state continuity.

Add the agreed audible instrumental background music and synchronized effects on separate layers. Let key actions cut through with local music dips. Keep audio editable.

Inspect a representative hero frame and key transition, fix visible issues, then render the full preview. Check exact copy, clipping, timing, continuity and audio. Report any verification you could not perform.

Deliver the editable AEP, source assets, audio stems and an H.264 MP4. Use local AE animation/rendering for this build; do not submit a cloud video-generation job.
```

The final sentence selects the production route. It does not eliminate model usage or the cost/allowance of generating new source images.
