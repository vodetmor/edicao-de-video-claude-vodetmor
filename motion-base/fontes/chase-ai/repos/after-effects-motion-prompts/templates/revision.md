# Revision prompt template

```text
Revise [PROJECT/VERSION]. Keep [APPROVED DESIGN, COPY, CHARACTER, DURATION OR AUDIO] unchanged.

At [TIMESTAMP/RANGE], the problem is [SPECIFIC OBSERVATION]. Change it to [DESIRED BEHAVIOR OR RESULT].
At [TIMESTAMP/RANGE], change the copy from [OLD] to [EXACT NEW COPY].

Keep the effects and background music on separate layers. Save a new version, inspect the changed moments and export an updated preview. Summarize what changed and any remaining limitation.
```

Examples based on this project:

- “The board reveal feels too static. Have the cards land first, then reveal their text, followed by the drawn accents and a character reaction. Shorten the camera handoffs while retaining time to read.”
- “Change the close to ‘Meet KITE. / Less busywork. / More momentum. / Join the waitlist’. Reveal those as separate beats and hold the completed layout for the last 2.4 seconds.”
- “The background music barely reads as music. Add an audible instrumental groove with warm bass, dry drums and a restrained synth motif. Keep the existing tactile effects and lower music briefly around approval and latch release.”

These examples are cleaned-up instructions based on the actual revisions, not verbatim quotes of every message in the session.
