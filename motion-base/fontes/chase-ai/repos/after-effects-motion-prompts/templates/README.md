# Reusable prompts

Use the templates in sequence, or begin with a reference video when you already know the motion language you want.

1. [Storyboard](storyboard.md): define the message, design, beats, exact copy and independently moving pieces.
2. [After Effects build](build.md): construct and animate the approved plan through the local bridge.
3. [Revision](revision.md): direct changes to copy, timing, composition and sound using concrete observations and timestamps.

[Reference video](reference-video.md) provides an alternative starting point: inspect an accessible clip, separate observation from inference and turn the result into a new production plan.

These are reusable templates from the working prompt kit. The revision examples are cleaned-up instructions based on actual edits, not verbatim quotes of every conversation message. A storyboard is a visual plan; it does not automatically contain separable artwork or editable AE layers.
