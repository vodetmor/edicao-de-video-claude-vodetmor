# Start your own animation

Replace the brackets and attach relevant design references. Use this first message to produce the visual plan. The second message commissions the After Effects build.

## 1. Storyboard and motion plan

```text
Act as a motion designer and art director. Create a storyboard and motion plan for an editable After Effects animation.

Topic or product: [NAME + ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION]
Audience: [WHO WILL WATCH]
Main takeaway: [ONE THING THEY SHOULD UNDERSTAND]
Desired action: [CTA, OR NONE]
Format: [DURATION, ASPECT RATIO, RESOLUTION, FPS]
Visual personality: [MATERIALS, COLORS, TYPOGRAPHY, CHARACTER]
Exact copy: [REQUIRED WORDS, OR PROPOSE CONCISE COPY]
References: [ATTACHED DESIGN, BRAND ASSETS, OR ACCESSIBLE VIDEO]
Motion priority: [E.G. QUICK CONNECTED TRANSITIONS, ACTIVE REVEALS, SHORT READING SETTLES]
Sound: [AUDIBLE INSTRUMENTAL MUSIC + SELECTIVE EFFECTS, OR SILENT]

Create one coherent visual world with a clear sequence. Each action should prepare or cause the next. Keep the same objects and design identities across the film.

If a video reference is supplied, inspect its actual motion through the ending. Map its approximate beats, framing, travel, holds and transitions to our subject. State any necessary retiming; do not claim to recover its original project or hidden prompts.

Deliver a short concept, exact-copy list, timed beat sheet, one visual storyboard contact sheet, and an asset plan. For each beat specify the main action, transition, reading opportunity and sound cue. All beats must fit the requested duration.

Separate native editable AE text/shapes from any imagery that needs preparation. A storyboard is a blueprint, not an automatically layered asset pack.

Show the storyboard and plan before starting After Effects production.
```

## 2. Build the approved plan

```text
Build the approved storyboard and motion plan in Adobe After Effects using the connected Higgsfield local bridge.

Verify the live connection, inspect the current project, and follow the integration's supplied AE instructions. Preserve existing work in a separate saved project/version.

Use our approved design, copy, duration, resolution and frame rate. Prepare only missing source artwork. Keep typography, UI, lines and simple graphics native and editable; keep required photographic or illustrated elements as separate source assets. Do not flatten the storyboard into the finished video.

Build a complete motion pass first. Connect camera travel and object actions, overlap related reveals where appropriate, and keep one dominant action at a time. Preserve brief reading opportunities without adding unintended dead pauses. Use the reference cadence when supplied.

Render and inspect the complete sequence. Fix framing, copy, clipping, crowded transitions, idle gaps, continuity, expression errors and the ending before adding final polish.

Add the agreed music and synchronized effects on separate layers. Keep the music present at the intended level and leave room for important cues. Use original or appropriately licensed audio and record its source.

Deliver the editable AEP, required assets, separate audio stems and H.264 MP4. Use local AE construction and rendering for this build. Report any relevant verification or editability limitations honestly.
```

## 3. Give a concrete revision

```text
Revise the current project and save a new version.

At [TIMESTAMP], change [SPECIFIC COPY, ACTION, FRAMING, TIMING OR SOUND].
The intended result is [WHAT SHOULD FEEL OR READ DIFFERENTLY].
Keep [APPROVED PARTS] consistent.

Render the revised sequence, compare the changed beat, and check its handoffs into the surrounding shots. Deliver the new preview and editable project.
```
