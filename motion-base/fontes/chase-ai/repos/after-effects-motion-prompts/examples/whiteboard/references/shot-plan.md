## Design and continuity invariants

- Preserve one master layout across all six frames. Close-ups are camera crops of the same board; do not redraw the notes with different aspect ratios, colors, text, folds, or tilts.
- Keep the eight final note positions stable. Do not recenter each note in the master board merely because it becomes the close-up subject.
- Keep each note's paper, folded corner, text, and reaction badge grouped so they enter and exit together. Text is progressively revealed within the same layout.
- Keep collaborator names and pointer colors consistent. Each pointer should relate to a nearby note and perform a readable action.
- Draw the yellow circle around Unified workspace only; keep it attached to that board location throughout the pullback.
- Preserve enough empty space for handwritten annotations and arrows. These details supply much of the board's character and should not be replaced with generic decoration.
- The top interface and left toolbar are visible in wide shots and fall outside close crops. Avoid a floating toolbar over the note close-ups.
- Keep the text sharp during holds. Blur belongs to fast travel and dispersal, not the reading frames.
- The source video has a black promotional surround, a Higgsfield overlay, and AE/chat panels beneath it. Our master board/storyboard should represent the finished board animation itself; those belong to a later demonstration edit if desired.
- Beat 06 must restore Beat 01 deliberately. Check note opacity, annotation strokes, cursor positions, zoom, and pan at both endpoints.
