# Chase AI — Your idea. In motion.

A 10-second, 1920×1080, 30 fps workflow bumper built with native After Effects text and shapes. One design moves from an idea into storyboard poses, separates into editable pieces, enters a timeline, receives a “Punchier.” revision and resolves into CHASE AI.

Use [the copy-ready build prompt](build-prompt.md) with [the storyboard](references/storyboard.png). The [cadence plan](references/cadence.md) explains the connected camera moves and overlapping reveals.

## What was actually used

[original-production-prompt.txt](original-production-prompt.txt) preserves the full prompt approved before the build. The copy-ready version changes only its two attachment paths and adds a usage note. The final project also received implementation corrections and render checks; this is not a guarantee of an identical result from one run.

The storyboard is the original visual study and prints an earlier 18-second plan. The approved production prompt and subsequent cadence plan replace those timings with **10 seconds**. The cadence document is a historical planning record; its final statement that the film had not yet been built describes that earlier stage.

Motion direction references the approximate rhythm of [Maddie’s whiteboard demo](https://x.com/madmadhere/status/2098498233035305427). The creator’s video, hidden keyframes and source project are not included. Attach an accessible reference clip if you want the agent to inspect motion; a social link alone may not provide playable video access.

## Reuse

1. Attach `references/storyboard.png` and keep the 10-second production timing authoritative.
2. Paste `build-prompt.md` into a local assistant connected to After Effects.
3. Change the wordmark, exact copy and palette for your own project, then inspect the rendered movement and revise it.

The final artwork is native AE text and shapes; the storyboard is not flattened into the movie. The timeline in the animation is a stylized graphic, not a screen recording of the actual editor. CHASE AI uses a plain typographic wordmark and a proposed charcoal/lime/cobalt/coral treatment for this demo.

The delivered soundtrack combines original local synthesis with Kenney CC0 samples. The prompt specifies audible background music and selective effects; it does not reproduce an audio waveform by itself. Fonts used in the delivered project were Impact, Arial and Arial Bold; font files are not included.
