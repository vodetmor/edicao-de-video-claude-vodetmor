# KITE — Pocket World v2 revision brief

This revision keeps Pocket World's clay/cardboard materials and restores the layered motion language of the earlier whiteboard: staggered text, drawn annotations, cursor reactions and brisk camera handoffs.

## What changes

The first version often revealed a complete card in one event and then held. Its long eased camera trips separated those events further. In v2, the material arrives, its title reveals a few frames later, status and drawn annotations follow, and the cursor or kite reacts. Quick camera handoffs connect those actions.

The visual story remains an AI Kanban announcement. The human still approves the copy before the task moves to Done; the blue design task becomes Ready while remaining in To do.

## Copy

- Opening: **Big ideas. Clear plan.**
- The user brief remains **Launch our website Friday.**
- The product action remains **Make a plan**.
- Elapsed-time cue: **Later · Draft ready**.
- Dependency: **Waiting for approved copy** / **Blocked**.
- Human action: **Approve**, followed by **Approved**.
- Progress statement: **Keep work moving.**
- Small collaborative reactions: **Looks good.** and/or **Your move.**, only where they reinforce the action.
- Closing: **Meet KITE. / Less busywork. / More momentum. / Join the waitlist**.

## Timing

| Time | Events |
| --- | --- |
| 0–1.5 s | Brief and short headline arrive with staggered reveals. Maya presses Make a plan at 1.10 s. |
| 1.5–3.6 s | Board arrives, card bodies land at short intervals, titles follow their landings, and the kite anticipates the next arrival. |
| 3.6–4.6 s | Flip the Later cue and move coral into Review. |
| 4.6–6.8 s | Reach the dependency view in 10–14 frames. Draw the cord, close the latch, reveal Blocked and its reason, then draw a small status circle. Hold the complete relationship for about one second. |
| 6.8–8.8 s | Maya enters during the camera handoff. Press Approve at 7.55 s, stamp a brief Approved reaction, then let the kite react. |
| 8.8–11.5 s | Coral reaches Done at 9.10 s; check at 9.18, latch opens at 9.35, blue Ready at 9.55. Draw an accent and reveal Keep work moving. |
| 11.5–15 s | Reveal the hero headline, supporting copy and CTA in distinct quick beats. Complete motion by 12.6 s for a 2.4-second read. |

## Production

Use the existing source material images with native editable After Effects elements. Keep 15-second 1920 × 1080 / 30 fps delivery. Physical objects retain selective stepped motion; camera movement is smooth. Drawn lines, copy and reactions supply overlapping activity. Preserve quiet reading holds and a clear visual hierarchy.

The v1 project and render stay available unchanged. Save v2 separately, with its own retimed original audio stems, source assets, native project and encoded preview. No new cloud image or video generation is needed for this revision.
