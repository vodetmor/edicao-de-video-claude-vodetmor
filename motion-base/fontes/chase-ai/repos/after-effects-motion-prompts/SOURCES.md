# Sources and scope

These are Chase AI demonstration films made through a local Codex → Higgsfield bridge → Adobe After Effects workflow. This is an independent example collection, not an official Adobe, OpenAI or Higgsfield package.

## Tooling

- [Higgsfield local After Effects bridge](https://github.com/higgsfield-ai/fnf-local-pluging-bridge-mcp#after-effects)
- [Higgsfield AI Motion Designer](https://higgsfield.ai/ai-motion-designer)
- [Adobe After Effects](https://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html)
- [Codex usage documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing)

Local AE editing and rendering through this bridge do not consume Higgsfield cloud-generation credits or require a Higgsfield account. The surrounding AI work still uses the applicable model allowance or API billing, and Adobe access is separate. Cloud image/video generation is a different service with its own usage rules.

## Motion reference

The Whiteboard example adapts the visible motion and design discussed in [Madi's Higgsfield whiteboard post](https://x.com/madmadhere/status/2098498233035305427). That clip also informed the approximate cadence of the Chase AI workflow bumper.

Our Whiteboard prompt is our executed adaptation. It is not the original creator's recovered full prompt. Original third-party videos and software/chat captures are not included. The reference boards included here are our generated planning images.

## Artwork

Chase AI Workflow and Whiteboard use native AE text and shapes in the finished videos. KITE combines four generated RGB material images with native live text, masks, lines, character details and animation. Its imagery represents a fixed view with shallow dimensional styling, not an orbitable 3D scene. The KITE artwork prompts requested transparency; the actual included files were isolated with native AE masks.

## Audio

Chase AI Workflow and KITE use original locally synthesized music and selected CC0 sound-library excerpts from Kenney. The Whiteboard video is silent.

- [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
- [Kenney RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio)

The videos are optimized H.264 previews of the completed films. Dimensions, duration and hashes are recorded in [media/provenance.json](media/provenance.json). The Whiteboard master was 4K; its repository preview is 1080p. No native AEPs, account configurations, private local paths, or execution logs are included in this prompt collection.
