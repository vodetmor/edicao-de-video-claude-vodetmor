Every content-lesson script, any subject (physics, humanities...), must show a progression of reasoning: each act opens with an explicit spoken transition announcing the next step ("agora vamos ver as questões que mais caem na prova sobre refração"), followed by a pause. The wording is free (question, contrast, "e se...", promise; not necessarily "agora vamos"): what matters is a felt progression of reasoning. An on-screen stamp alone is not enough.
**Why:** Vítor, 2026-10-05: the optics lesson was "muito bom" but felt like concept, concept, exam with no turning points; the oblique-launch lesson had them and worked better.
**How to apply:** write the transitions into the script before the plan; give each one a planned pause in tempo.py and a visual turn. Details in LICOES-MOTION.md "Roteiro de aula: progressão de raciocínio". Related: [[feedback-roteiro-voz]], [[aula-otica]].
