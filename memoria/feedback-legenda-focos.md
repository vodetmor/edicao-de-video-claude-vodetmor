Rules Vítor set on 2026-10-05 for videos with his face (bastidores round 2):
- The caption must NEVER cover his face. With a split screen it sits on the seam between the panels; with his face full screen it goes on his chest. One line, same size and place for the whole video.
- At most 3 focal points at a time: top screen, his speech (face), caption. No extra trackers or cards over his face.
- Keep his face centered in its panel. Split screen is HALF and HALF (960/960), not 2/3 for the top.
- Cut silences, breaths and "é/hum" hesitations without breaking the video (jump cut + alternating zoom).
- A making-of must show the actual finished video, with its sound, right after the hook.
- Use real logos for any app mentioned.

**Why:** he said more than 3 focal points overloads the viewer, and a caption over his face "fica paia".
**How to apply:** check every keyframe against these rules before rendering. Recipe in motion-base/LICOES-MOTION.md (bastidores rodada 2). See [[feedback-entrega-celular]].
