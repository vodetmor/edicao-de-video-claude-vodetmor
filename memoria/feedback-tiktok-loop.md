For every personal-channel (TikTok/Instagram) motion video, not client work:
1. Last frame visually equal to (or the moment just before) the first frame, so viewers loop and rewatch.
2. Text never crossed by lines and never on a background of its own color; check every key frame.
3. Sound and image composed together (music + synced SFX carry the video); also for his talking-head videos.
4. When he sends work overnight and says so, plan + keyframes + render are pre-approved: deliver the finished video; he asks for redo if needed.
**Why:** retention (curious viewers rewatch) and his complaint about covered text in the first lesson.
**How to apply:** see [[aula-otica]] for the loop technique (light turns off, camera returns to frame-0 pose).
