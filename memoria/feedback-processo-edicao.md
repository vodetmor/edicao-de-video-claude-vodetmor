On 2026-09-30 Vítor rated the narrated VSL Play ad 10/10 ("incrível, estou impressionado") and asked that this level of editing be the standard for everything: client videos, his personal content and other edits.
He does not want Claude to merely copy an editing style. Every edit follows the process used on [[anuncio-vslplay]]: gather references, write a SPEC (measured parameters), design an architecture, write a plan, fix all parameters, then edit, then a fresh critic pass.
**Why:** the process, not a single style, is what produced the quality he liked.
**How to apply:** before any new video work, build references + SPEC + architecture + plan (see motion-base/TEMPLATE-BRIEF.md, DESIGN-SYSTEM.md, LICOES-MOTION.md) even when a style reference exists. Related: [[design-system-video]], [[motion-base-knowledge]].
