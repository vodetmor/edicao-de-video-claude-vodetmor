Vítor, 2026-10-09 on the Rei da VSL price ads v1: "muito rápido, informação demais, não dá para ler nem ver; o gancho dos 3 s não fisgou; a caixa 3D aparece 1 segundo". He said the design was good and he loved the 3D. Up to 1 min is fine.

**Why:** we had copied the cut rate of silent showreels (one change every ~1 s). That doesn't work under a voiceover with on-screen text.

**How to apply:**
- One idea per scene. Text stays on screen long enough to read it twice.
- UPDATED 2026-10-10: the client (Deidson) wants NO inserted pauses: see [[feedback-capa-criativo]]. Readability comes from less information per scene.
- The 3 s hook is one strong, legible, moving object, not three cuts.
- Keep 3D hero pieces on screen for 3 s or more.
- Strip decorative layers (HUD, mono notes, echoes).
- Offer more real 3D (Blender) moments.

Details are in motion-base/LICOES-MOTION.md, "Legibilidade acima do ritmo medido". Related: [[criativos-precos-rei-da-vsl]], [[feedback-processo-edicao]].
