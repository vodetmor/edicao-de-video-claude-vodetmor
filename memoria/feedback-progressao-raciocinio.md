Vítor (2026-10-05, after the optics lesson): the optics video was good but lacked progression of reasoning. The oblique-launch lesson had it ("agora vamos para as três perguntas que mais caem na prova", with a pause, changing the line of thought). Every content video (any subject, including humanities) must move in clear stages, each opened by a transition (any wording or form he and Claude find best, not necessarily "agora vamos") and a short pause/chapter beat, never just concept -> exam question -> concept.
Also for question videos: redraw the official figure as vectors and build/focus it part by part while he reads; use 3D where it explains a physical idea (e.g. constant flow filling a cube).
**Why:** viewers follow the reasoning and stay to the end.
**How to apply:** scripts and edits; see [[questao-tangente]], [[feedback-roteiro-questao]], [[aula-lancamento-obliquo]].
