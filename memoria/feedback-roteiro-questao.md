When a personal-channel video solves an ENEM/exam question, the script must first read the official statement and the command line ("A expressão... é") aloud, quoting it, with a short comment after each part (what each term means), and only then a quick explanation. Keep it concise (he rejected a 4:30 wordy version) and never promise a time ("em 30 segundos") the video itself contradicts. When he narrates himself, write numbers and formulas as digits/symbols (he reads them fine).
**Why:** Vítor's feedback on the tangent question script, 2026-10-05: without reading the question it "não ficou didático".
**How to apply:** every question-solving script; see [[questao-tangente]], [[feedback-roteiro-voz]].
