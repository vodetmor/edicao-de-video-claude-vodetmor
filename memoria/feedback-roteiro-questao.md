When a personal-channel video solves an ENEM/exam question: (1) present the question first (summarized, quoting key parts and the command; he said reading it in full was not needed); (2) the explanation needs a guiding thread ("costura"): one central idea and numbered steps a beginner can follow, with an on-screen roadmap; teach the prerequisite concepts from zero with visual metaphors (he wants a total beginner to follow and stay to the end); too short is as bad as wordy (v1 4:30 wordy rejected, v2/v3 ~2 min "not simple, no path"). Never promise a time ("em 30 segundos") the video contradicts. When he narrates himself, write numbers/formulas as digits/symbols.
**Why:** Vítor's four rounds of feedback on the tangent question script, 2026-10-05.
**How to apply:** every question-solving script; see [[questao-tangente]], [[feedback-roteiro-voz]].
