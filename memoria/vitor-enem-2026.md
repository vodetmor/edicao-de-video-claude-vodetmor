2026-10-04 mentor talk (thread "dilema faculdade vs dinheiro"): Vítor, 3rd year of high school, from DF; target = Engenharia de Computação at UnB (entry via PAS 3 at end of year + SISU). Digital stays in maintenance mode until PAS, not just ENEM. Income: R$900/month allowance from his grandmother + R$400 + % of offers from his boss's work.
PAS escore bruto: PAS1 35, PAS2 42; PAS essays 9.7 and 9.9 (near max). PAS3 weighs ~50%; last Eng. Computação cutoff ~103 argumento final (ampla). Advice: essays down to 1/week, more Mat/Nat; after ENEM focus only on PAS3.

Advice given: ENEM first until the exam. Split study time Mat 40% / Natureza 35% / Ling+Hum 25%, plus 2 essays a week. Boss work capped at 1 h/day, no new projects. One channel video a week built from what he studied. Postpone the AI video-editing course until after ENEM (audience first, then a pre-sale).
**How to apply:** when he proposes big new digital projects before the ENEM, remind him of this plan. Related: [[feedback-organizar-ideias]].
