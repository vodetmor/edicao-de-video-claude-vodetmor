Vítor (2026-10-14, MedicMais video): "toda vez que você for citar alguma outra empresa, coloque o logo dela, a identidade dela, porque o cliente não vê o que está escrito, ele vê a marca." Google search = a bar with the Google logo; "feed" = the real Instagram identity (a print of the actual profile, animated); WhatsApp, Maps, Google Meu Negócio = official logos. Real physical interactions get their real sound (the NFC payment "tim").
**Why:** the viewer recognizes brands instantly; plain words in pills don't register.
**How to apply:** official logo files in motion-base/logos (wikimedia-*, gstatic-*; see LEIA-ME). Never redraw them. This is in LICOES-MOTION too.
