Built 2026-10-05 after Vítor asked for more music variety than Kevin MacLeod, a new track for each next video.
/mnt/project-files/motion-base/musica/: CATALOGO.md (10 tracks each for aula, fala, gancho, final, leve; license + ready credit line), faixas/, previas/ (30 s), usos.csv (track used per video + his reaction), fonte/ (coleta.py, analise.py with CLAP, seleciona.py, pagina.py). Listening page: https://claude.ai/artifact/JPMU8pKeNsfXMcbxRWJyHr
Sources: Scott Buckley (CC BY 4.0, best quality), ccMixter (CC BY, needs Referer header), Jamendo (only CC BY; most of it is NC/ND), Freesound (CC0/CC BY). Pixabay/Chosic/Uppbeat/Musopen blocked from cloud.
Gotcha: CLAP needs laion/larger_clap_music_and_speech + transformers 4.46; transformers 5 or larger_clap_music gives ~0 scores.
**Why:** he liked Inspired (aula, "perfeita"), Deliberate Thought (fala); Wallpaper sounded "de piada" in a lesson.
**How to apply:** pick from the catalog by mood, check usos.csv (no track from last 3 videos, no same artist twice in a row), log use + credit in ESTADO.md and post description. Rule also in LICOES-MOTION.md. See [[feedback-roteiro-voz]].
