2. His ElevenLabs narration is slower than edge-tts: follow his pace (do not squeeze his natural silences), give pauses at visual moments, and let the music rise smoothly in those pauses.
**Why:** Vítor's feedback on the optics lesson, 2026-10-05 ("de resto tudo está perfeito").
