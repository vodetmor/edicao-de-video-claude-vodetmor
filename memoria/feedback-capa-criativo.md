Deidson (client, Rei da VSL), 2026-10-10, relayed by Vítor:
1. COVER: every ad creative must show the full key piece (price, offer, title), finished and legible, on its very first frame. Never an empty screen, never an element still entering. The ad platform uses frame 0 as the thumbnail, and the team tells creatives apart in campaigns by it. Creatives in a series need distinct covers (background color + number).
2. NARRATION: keep it direct and dynamic, with no gaps between sentences. Trim natural silences to about 0.18 s (esticar.py). Add a 0.25–0.35 s pause only where the lead must look (an example on screen, a list building, "este vídeo é um exemplo"). He suggested speeding up 1.2–1.4x; trimming silence keeps the voice natural, so prefer that.

**Why:** finding the creative in Ads Manager, plus retention. This overrides the slower pacing from Vítor's round-4 feedback ([[feedback-legibilidade-criativo]]): keep readability by putting less information on screen, not by pausing the voice.
**How to apply:** check frame 0 alone before every ad delivery. Standards live in motion-base/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §6 and LICOES-MOTION.md ("Capa no quadro 0"). Related: [[criativos-precos-rei-da-vsl]].
