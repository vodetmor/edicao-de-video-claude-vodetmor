Vítor (2026-10-09, Rei da VSL creatives plan): liked the design ("bonito demais, tá foda") but the 12-page PDF was too long and over-explained: "ninguém tem tempo de ler tudo isso". Every approval document must be OBJECTIVE and didactic: show what each video will be (keyframes) and the idea of each part in one line, plus a sound/audiovisual page (music + SFX) when relevant. No long reasoning, measured-reference essays or SPEC tables in the client-facing PDF; those stay in internal files (SPEC.md, ESTADO.md).
**Why:** the PDF is forwarded to clients (Dhay etc.) to approve; they skim.
**How to apply:** approval PDF ≈ cover + 1-2 pages per video (keyframes with time + one-line caption) + 1 sound page + 1 "to approve" checklist. Keep the brand design. Related: [[feedback-quadros-chave]], [[criativos-precos-rei-da-vsl]].
