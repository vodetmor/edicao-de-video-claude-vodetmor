Vítor (2026-10-02, GLSL video thread): even when he approves an architected plan, he wants a gate before the full build: the storytelling and the keyframes (stills of each planned key moment, rendered for real) sent for his approval, "igual você fez lá com o VSL Play". Pause the full render at that gate, continue after his OK.
**Why:** he likes plans, but judges the video by seeing the key frames before time goes into the full render.
**How to apply:** plan PDF -> build enough to render ~12 real keyframes -> one PDF board (story in acts + each frame with time, spoken word, what happens) -> wait for OK -> full render, sound, critic. Related: [[feedback-processo-edicao]], [[estudio-motor]].
